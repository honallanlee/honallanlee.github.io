I’m Allan Lee, a Test Development Engineer at Qualcomm in Cambridge. I work on mixed-signal SoCs across digital, audio, radio and power management, from design for test before tape-out to high-volume production. I trained in biomedical engineering, and I’m still hooked on audio, medical devices and biomedical ASICs.

ECA Promotion Fair system

A system for running a school’s club sign-up fair, from scan to payment. I started it in 2016 as student association treasurer, on Google services. In 2026 I rebuilt it as a Next.js and Postgres web app.

  • One phone camera session reads a student’s slip, then each club label. Handheld barcode scanners work too.
  • Each form’s club rules and every price are checked again on the server, and the database itself refuses a second payment.
  • Tested against a real Postgres: twenty cashiers pressing Pay at once take one payment, and a rehearsal puts 990 students through ten counters.
